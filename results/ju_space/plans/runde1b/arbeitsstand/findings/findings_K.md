# Unverified review findings for K (finder stage only; verification and fixes were stopped to save quota)

## find:part K:validity

Summary: The validity review found 14 problems in part K: 4 high, 7 medium, 3 low. Most of the attractor (B) component rests on estimators that the common-random-number twin design biases.

- **validity-1 (high), K6e carry.** The primary carry K_TOK = E_2/E_1 is not like-for-like. Segment 1 contains the shared seed and the positions before the first token flip, which contribute exact zeros; segment 2 lies wholly after the onset. This inflates K by 1.14-1.90. In a simulation with the plan's own structure, a decaying per-position carry of 0.75 reads 'self-sustaining' in 61-100 % of runs.
- **validity-2 (high), 'length artifact' strings.** These strings (K6b(i), K6h, K8c) are reached by conditioning on entropy, margin, top-1 probability and evidence of the very decision being scored, or on step difficulty. That is not length, so falsifiers 4 and 8 and the A0/M0 readings misstate the user's central question.
- **validity-3 (high), K6e-b bistability.** Undiverged twin pairs give z = 0 exactly. Run on monostable data with this point mass, the plan's own verdict function says 'bistable' in 83-100 % of cases.
- **validity-4 (high), 'no within-item bistability'.** This rule (share of z > 2 below 10 %) tests for no shift and no skew, not for no second mode. The plan's own bound2.out shows pass rates of 0.00-0.21 for skewed or shifted monostable nulls. B-, falsifiers 6-7 and composite row 5 are therefore practically unreachable.
- **validity-5 (medium), K8b FULL and K6e TOK+KV.** Both keep the perturbed K/V in the cache, so the perturbation is never released, against the plan's own attractor definition.
- **validity-6 (medium), repeated values counted once.** This scoring rule makes q(s) rise by +0.02 to +0.07 logit/step at constant reading quality. It also biases K6g and K6e-c.
- **validity-7 (medium), K6a's V2 null.** V2 sits at the ceiling, so 'generic rollout drift' is unreachable and plain drift is reported as 'beyond the null state'.
- **validity-8 (medium), RS20 matching.** RS20 is matched on REF-K16 damage, not on token flip rate in the lens frame. Timing alone shifts log K by about 2 planned SDs.
- **validity-9 (medium), falsifiers that mean something else.** These are: falsifier 2 (a failure of the substitute instrument), K6e-d (context length, not chain length), K9 (an existential framing), and row 1 for K3a 'undecided'.
- **validity-10 (medium), K8a falsifier 9.** It has no random-set or CETT-normalisation reference.
- **validity-11 (medium), K10c.** CL17's chain is selected by the true first digit of the answer, which favours the chain readout by construction.
- **validity-12 to -14 (low).** H activity is read without regard to the sign of z. K10b's radial perturbation direction is invisible to the next block under RMSNorm and per-head q/k normalisation. The atlas metrics the user asked for (damage per firing, simulatability, mode shares) need matched references if added. K does not yet contain them.

Every finding comes with a concrete fix. Most are analysis-only or cost-neutral. The one with a cost is calibrating RS20 to the lens-frame flip rate, about 5 min of work.
