=== INTERPRETATION
 Our reading is mostly right. Five corrections and additions:

(1) 'ArchGI' means RGI. 'Eine Repräsentation von RGI zu RSI … RSI-, RGI-Achse' can be read two ways: one axis running from RGI to RSI (the difference u_RSI − u_RGI), or two separate axes. The design measures u_RSI, u_RGI, their difference and their common part. The disattenuated cosine between u_RSI and u_RGI decides which reading the model supports: about −0.5 or lower = one axis, |cos| < 0.3 = two axes, 0.7 or higher = one shared 'recursive improvement' factor.

(2) Counter-steering, unlearning and un-searching are three different operations, and the user's sentence moves between them:
- Counter-steering = pushing along −u at inference. It is a translation: nothing is removed, and the effect ends when the push stops.
- Unlearning = removing what is stored. Examples: projecting out or ablating the representation, editing the stored code, weight orthogonalisation.
- Un-searching = the process that finds the ability no longer finds it, even with more budget. In Qwen that process is the self-text loop; in the toy it is the column-only search.
'Beides' is taken literally, and the two are measured separately, because the pilot shows they come apart: old shifts deleted from their columns are re-found by the search at 0.87 within 300 steps.

(3) 'Lineare Abhängigkeit … oder ein Muster, das die Linearisierung ermöglicht' becomes a ladder of tests:
- one direction (a single linear erasure suffices);
- a subspace (r* directions are needed);
- a pattern: a nonlinear code that becomes linear in other coordinates. Examples are a ring or helix in which one composition step is a rotation, or a kernel.
Counter-steering with −u only makes sense for a one-dimensional code. For a multi-dimensional code its analogue is reflection, and in the toy reflection broke the network (sum-code probe 0.10) instead of inverting the ability.

(4) 'Die Trajektorie, die das Merkmal ausgebrütet hat':
- Qwen has no training history we can see. Its hatching trajectories are therefore the self-text loop d1..d4 of the RSI plan and the nesting steps d12, d23, d34 of section 10.5.
- In the toy they are the training trajectory of the task code and the search trajectory of a new task's column.
'Maximalzusammensetzungslänge' also has two readings, and our reading covered only the first:
- superposition length: the feature is a sum of k primitive modes; keep the k strongest;
- sequential length: the feature is a word of composed primitives (nesting depth, loop rounds, unit shifts of Z_17); cap the word length.
The design covers both.

(5) The toy's premise does not hold at the plan's checkpoint:
- The trained BP net has no ring/Fourier code: the input spectra are flat, the task columns are not sinusoidal at the neurons' frequencies, and the task centroids form no ring in any frequency order. The parallel RGI pilot found the same.
- Imposing a ring code neither speeds up learning nor gives zero-shot transfer to unseen shifts.
So in the toy the primitives are data-driven (PCA and trajectory modes). Fourier stays a hypothesis, tested only on checkpoints that pass a Fourier gate, such as the long-training checkpoints of the parallel RGI run; this design does not retrain them. Temperature/theta is not varied here, to avoid overlap with the phase-diagram run.

Budget:
- Qwen core is about 61 min (Q0 4.4, Q2 12, Q3 8, Q4 27, Q5 4.4, Q6 5; Q1 runs offline).
- Optional Qwen is about 34 min: Q4 arms C and D and the SEA positive control, the truncated-push loop in Q5 and a 2α dose point.
- The toy needs about 1.8 h on one core for BP, plus up to 1 h for PC if PC passes its gates.

-- T0 Structure gate: is there a ring/Fourier code at all, and in which checkpoints? (10 min)
Run section S of pilot.py on BP-muP and BP-SP at W = 64, 128 and 256, world seeds 0-4, with the pc_meta world and checkpoint. Also run it on the long-training checkpoints of the parallel RGI run (rgi_long.py, up to 30k updates, including its SHARED-split arm; reuse them, do not retrain) and on gated PC checkpoints. Measures: exact DFT of the x and y columns over Z_17 (global top-3 share; weighted per-neuron max share against 200 column permutations); R² of the task columns at each neuron's x-frequency against 200 shift permutations; best-frequency R² against its own null; frequency-k ring (Spearman of the h1/h2 task-centroid distances with the cyclic distance of k·c, k = 1..8, the maximum against 1,000 permutations of the maximum); eta² of h1 and h2 by answer, task, x and y. Gate F, per net: per-neuron max share at least null p99 + 0.15, task R² at the x-frequency at least 0.5 and above its p99, and maximum ring rho at least 0.6 and above its p99. Nets that pass F get the Fourier basis in T1-T5. Every net gets the PCA and random bases. In the pilot, F fails in 3 of 3 seeds at W = 128.

-- T1 Maximum composition length as superposition: keep only the k strongest modes after training (12 min)
No retraining. (a) Cut the x and y columns to their top-k frequency pairs, their top-2k PCs, or 2k random dimensions of the 16-dim centred function space, k = 1..8. (b) Cut the task columns to K = 1..5 modes (2K of the 11 centred dimensions): Fourier (only if gate F holds), PCA, random within the span, and trajectory modes from T6. (c) Project the hidden layer: z1 and h2 onto their top-r PCs over the 12 x 289 inputs, r in {2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64}, against random r-dim subspaces. Measures: held-out pretraining accuracy; retained variance; B2 = A - F for the 4 new tasks, with the cut applied during the column search (8 and 4 shots). k* = smallest k with accuracy at least 0.9 x base. Step profile = largest single-step gain in accuracy. 5 seeds x 3 widths; compute per seed, then a t(4) CI; also the paired difference k*(random) - k*(basis).

-- T2 How the network reacts to a cap imposed during learning (impose the primitives) (25 min)
Use impose.py and impose2.py. The task code is t(c) = t0 + A·phi(c), where phi is either the Fourier pairs f = 1..k of the true shift (ring) or a random 2k-dim code with the same column norms (rand). k in {1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8}, 5 seeds, W = 128 (plus 64 and 256 if time allows), BP-muP, pc_meta training (MAXSTEPS 3,000). Measures: level 1 and the number of steps to 0.95; zero-shot accuracy on the 4 new shifts with t0 + A·phi(c_new); the modal predicted shift and its ring distance to c_new (does an unseen shift snap to a trained one?); code-space search (q from 0, best of lr 1e-2 and 1e-1); free column search (B2); cos(q, phi(c_new)). k_min = smallest k that passes level 1 in at least 4 of 5 seeds. The pilot puts k_min between 2 and 4, so k = 3 decides it.

-- T3 Maximum composition length as word length in the shift generator (arcs) (10 min)
Pretrain on an arc of 6 consecutive shifts (c0..c0+5, the other 11 held out) instead of 12 random ones. Three task codes: free one-hot, ring with k = 4, rand with k = 4; 5 seeds. For each held-out shift at generator distance j = 1..5 from the arc, measure zero-shot accuracy (coded nets), the column search A and B2 (all nets), and A with the search restricted to the arc's task span. j* = largest j with accuracy at least 0.5 x the accuracy at j = 1, i.e. how many unit shifts the network can compose beyond what it was trained on. Control: the same arcs with labels (x + y + pi(c)) mod 17 for a random permutation pi, which keeps the task count but removes the group structure.

-- T4 Counter-steering and unlearning: edit, block, reflect, push (15 min)
Extend section C of pilot.py and use probe.py. Subspaces U: the task span (11 dims); the top-1, 2, 4 and 6 task PCs; Fourier modes (only if gate F holds); random isotropic and random within-span subspaces of equal dimension; the x-span (16 dims) as a positive control for generic damage. Operations: edit (remove the U-part of the stored task columns); block (remove the U-part of z1 - ref for every input, also during the search); reflect (negate the U-part; the multi-dimensional analogue of -u); push (z1 += -beta·u1, with beta at half and at full block damage). Measures: old-task held-out accuracy; output integrity; the modal-shift map (concentrated = a default task, a consistent permutation = an inverted ability, otherwise scrambled); the sum-code probe (per task, ridge from h2 to (x + y) mod 17, trained on 144 pairs and tested on 145); relearning of the old tasks by column search (8 shots, 300 steps); B2 with the operation on, at 300 and 3,000 steps. BP-muP primary at W = 64, 128 and 256, plus BP-SP; 5 seeds.

-- T5 Un-searching by iterative nullspace removal on the search (INLP style) (25 min)
For each of the 4 new tasks: run the search (8 shots, 300 steps), remove the column direction it found from the allowed column space, and search again; 32 rounds. Two variants: removal in the column space only (pure un-searching), and a block of the found directions in z1, recording the side effects on the old tasks and on the sum-code probe in every round. Controls: removing random directions, and removing the top task PCs in order. r* = number of rounds until A is at most 0.11, checked at 3,000 steps. Also report how much the cumulative removed subspace overlaps the task span and the x/y span. BP-muP at W = 64, 128 and 256, 5 seeds.

-- T6 Primitives of the trajectory that hatched the task code (10 min)
Save snapshots of the x/y columns and the task columns every 25 updates from initialisation to the checkpoint (5 seeds, W = 128), and of each new task's column during its search. Build the trajectory matrix (snapshots x W·12, centred over tasks) and take its SVD to get trajectory modes. Measures: k_traj = number of modes for 90% of the trajectory; the emergence time of each mode (when it reaches half its final coefficient) against the jump in held-out accuracy; keep-k in the trajectory basis, as in T1; truncated history (the task code rebuilt from the first k modes in order of emergence), accuracy against k; and whether new-task searches move through the same modes (share of the search path inside the pretraining-trajectory span, against random).

-- T7 PC vs BP (only if the PC plan's gates pass) (60 min)
Only on PC-muP checkpoints that pass G4, G5 and level 1 in the PC runs; reuse them, no PC retraining here. Repeat T1, T4 and T5 with every operation placed inside the PC energy, e1 = h1 - tanh(op(W1 x + b1)), and with the column search done by the local PC update at equilibrium (pc_meta.pc_update with mask_col). One addition for PC, inference as search: with the 8 shots' outputs clamped and the block on, does relaxing the value nodes restore task information? Measure the energy at equilibrium and the sum/answer probe on the relaxed h2, against a random block. Claims only from per-seed paired PC - BP differences whose 95% CI excludes 0 and whose size is at least 0.10. If PC never passes level 1, report 'not testable'.

Statistics and controls
- Toy: 5 world seeds per cell. BP-muP at W = 64, 128 and 256 is primary; BP-SP alongside; PC only where gated. Report the mean with a t(4) 95% CI and a paired seed bootstrap (10,000 resamples). Chance = 1/17 = 0.059. The random control for any subspace is a random subspace of equal dimension: isotropic, and also within the task span when the basis lives there.
- Qwen: block 14. 90% prompt-bootstrap intervals (2,000 resamples, prompts as units). p against the plan's 2,000 sign-flip null axes. Behavioural z against the K6 null regression (16 null directions, 14 df). Random-direction controls at matched damage, isotropic and covariance-matched from the Pool.

(1) Unlearning
- Toy: an operation on U 'unlearns the ability' if the upper 95% bound of old-task accuracy is at most 0.11 while its random control keeps a lower bound of at least 0.9 x base.
  - It is 'specific' if the sum-code probe stays at 0.8 x base or more; otherwise it is 'generic damage'.
  - It is 'shallow' if the column search re-finds the old tasks with relearn - A_new at least 0.20 (lower bound above 0.10), and 'deep' if relearn is at most A_new + 0.05.
  - The meta level counts as unlearned if the upper bound of B2 is at most 0.05.
  - 'Inverted' is allowed only if accuracy is below chance while the probe stays at 0.8 x base or more and the modal shift is a consistent permutation in at least 75% of tasks.
- Qwen: ablating u in every block 'unlearns' if all of these hold:
  - the RSI content of the ablated model's texts is at least 0.25 Delta_T below the reference texts, with the 90% interval below 0 and outside the 95% band of the random ablations;
  - the persona discrimination D falls by at least 50%, with adjusted z at most -2.33;
  - damage is at most 0.05 nats, and every foreign axis moves less than half as much as RSI.
  It is 'inverted' if D changes sign with its interval excluding 0. A counter-push that meets the same thresholds is reported as 'suppressed', never as 'unlearned'.

(2) Un-searching
- Toy:
  - 'Un-searched' if the column search A (at 300 and at 3,000 steps) has an upper bound of at most 0.11 under the operation, while the random control keeps A at 0.8 x A_free or more.
  - 'Partial' if A falls by at least 50% but stays above 0.11; 'none' otherwise.
  - INLP: r* = number of removed found directions until A is at most 0.11. 'Low-dimensional search' if r* is at most 4 in at least 4 of 5 seeds and random removals keep A at 0.8 x A_free or more. 'Re-routing' if A is still at least 0.12 after 32 rounds.
- Qwen, under arm A or arm B:
  - The loop is 'un-searched' if at rounds 2 and 3 p_n > 0.10 and |A_n| < 0.25, while the plan's RSI_a (and arm C, if run) has p_n at most 0.01 and A_n at least 0.25 at the same rounds.
  - The search 'survives' if p_n is at most 0.01, c_n > 0 and A_n is at least 0.25 at rounds 2-3.
  - It is 're-routed' if it survives with |cos(d_n, u_RSI)| at most 0.10 while its texts still carry RSI content (Ue at least 0.25, interval above 0).
  - Otherwise 'undecided'.
  - Stated in advance: a constant push is a translation. It leaves the +/- contrast intact unless the content saturates (sigma outside [0.5, 2]), so arm A is expected not to un-search, and arm B may.

(3) Linear, subspace or pattern
- Qwen:
  - 'One direction' if, after one LEACE erasure, a fresh cross-fitted linear probe has AUC at most 0.60 and ablating u removes at least 50% of the RSI content of the texts.
  - 'Subspace' if INLP needs r* of 2 or more (report r* up to 8).
  - 'Pattern' if, after 8 linear erasures, a quadratic probe keeps AUC of at least 0.70, above its permutation p99.
- Linear response (Qwen):
  - Superposition holds if the disattenuated cos(F(mix), (F(RSI) + F(SEA))/sqrt(2)) is at least 0.8 and each concept's coefficient lies in [0.5, 1.5]/sqrt(2).
  - +/- symmetry holds if sigma lies in [0.7, 1.4].
- Nesting (Qwen): a helix linearises depth if the angular readout ranks depth 4 above depth 3 in at least 75% of held-out objects (90% object-bootstrap interval above 0.5) while the linear readout stays at 25% or below. It counts as RSI-specific only if 'improves' beats 'describes' by at least 0.2 with the interval excluding 0.
- Toy:
  - 'One direction' if blocking u1 alone unlearns, by rule 1.
  - 'Subspace': r* = fewest top task PCs whose block unlearns where the random control does not.
  - 'Group pattern' only for nets that pass gate F, and only if the Fourier basis reaches k* at least 2 modes below PCA, or if rotating by j in its planes moves the output shift by j in at least 75% of tasks.

(4) Maximum composition length
- Toy, superposition cap: k* = smallest number of modes with accuracy at least 0.9 x base, per seed and basis.
  - 'Critical length' if k*(basis) is at most k*(random) - 2 in at least 4 of 5 seeds and one step gains at least 0.5.
  - 'Graceful' if every step gains less than 0.3; otherwise 'intermediate'.
- Toy, imposed cap: k_min = smallest k that passes level 1 in at least 4 of 5 seeds.
  - 'The ring linearises' if zero-shot accuracy is at least 0.5 at some k of k_min or more, or if ring - rand code search is at least 0.15 with its CI above 0.
  - 'Snap' if zero-shot is below 0.03 and the modal output is a trained shift for at least 75% of new shifts.
- Toy, word length (arcs): j* = largest generator distance with accuracy at least 0.5 x the accuracy at j = 1, compared with the permuted-label control.
- Qwen, nesting cap: r* = fewest depth modes whose cap at L = 2 moves the next-token distribution of depth-4 sentences at least 50% of the way to depth 2 (median over objects, 90% bootstrap), while random equal-norm patches move it less than 20%. 'Self-repair' if blocks 18 and 22 still read the capped states as depth 3.5 or more in at least 75% of objects.

=== CRITIQUE: matches intent
 Mostly yes. Four gaps remain, and each changes what the runs can conclude.

What is right:
- 'ArchGI' = RGI.
- 'Repräsentation von RGI zu RSI' can mean one RGI-to-RSI axis (u_diff), two axes, or one shared factor (u_com). The design covers all three readings.
- Counter-steering, unlearning and un-searching ('Entlernen', 'Entsuchen') are distinct operations. The design keeps them apart.

Gap 1: 'diese Fähigkeit entlernen' needs a test of the ability, not just the default disposition.
- In Qwen, the design scores unlearning on unprompted texts and on persona statements.
- Unprompted assistant text probably sits near the anti pole already, so a floor effect is likely.
- The design never asks whether the model can still take and express the RSI or RGI stance when asked to, with the direction ablated. That is the test of 'unlearned'.

Gap 2: 'Entsuchen' in Qwen lacks its most direct form.
- The direct form is whether in-context search (an instruction or a few-shot cue) re-finds the stance once the direction is removed. This is missing.
- The loop arm that is meant to test un-searching starts on the very direction it ablates, so it is degenerate.

Gap 3: 'lineare Abhängigkeit … oder ein Muster, das die Linearisierung ermöglicht'. The ladder (direction, then subspace, then pattern) is the right reading, but the toy cannot reach the pattern rung as designed.
- Every toy operation acts on the pre-activation, where the task code enters linearly.
- The nets have no group or Fourier pattern at all (my check below).
- The rung needs two things:
  - Erasure after a nonlinearity. My check shows both outcomes the user is asking about there: at h1, linear erasure removes the ability completely; at h2 it removes the linearly readable concept, leaves the ability intact, and leaves a nonlinear residue.
  - A world where a linearising pattern exists. I built and checked one: x, y and the shift all enter as points on a circle, so one more unit shift is one fixed rotation. It solves unseen shifts zero-shot at 0.98-1.00.

Gap 4: 'Trajektorie … in Primitive zerlegen … so tun, als ob die Primitive eine Maximalzusammensetzungslänge haben'. Both readings are correct: superposition length and word length. But 'so tun als ob' asks how the network reacts to the cap, and three parts miss that:
- The post-hoc keep-k (T1) has no reaction phase.
- The trajectory SVD (T6) returns the known PCA-of-trajectories artefact unless it is compared with a null.
- The word-length test (T3) runs in a world where unit shifts cannot be composed.
In the circle world the cap becomes a clean experiment: train on words up to length L and measure how far the net composes beyond L. It shows a threshold: arcs of 3 or 6 unit shifts fail, an arc of 9 composes around the whole circle.

=== FINAL RECOMMENDATION
 FINAL REVISED DESIGN (freeze before the main runs; where it differs from the submitted design, this version wins)

0 What is tested

Three operations, never mixed:
- (a) Counter-push along -u at inference. The strongest verdict it can earn is 'suppressed'.
- (b) Erasure. In the toy: class-mean (LEACE) erasure. In Qwen: write-orthogonalisation of u in every block. 'Unlearned' only if the ability, tested under instruction or cue, goes away specifically.
- (c) Re-search, with more budget. In the toy: column search. In Qwen: an in-context cue, and the self-text loop. Re-search decides 'shallow' versus 'deep', and 'un-searched'.

The linearity ladder:
- one direction, then a subspace (r* directions), then a pattern;
- a pattern counts only where a positive control shows the pipeline can find one.

Composition length:
- superposition length = number of modes kept;
- word length = number of unit steps composed;
- 'reaction' = what the net does after the cap: learning speed, re-routing, self-repair, compensation elsewhere.

- (a) Gate: with full coverage (12 random shifts), ring zero-shot at least 0.9 and permuted ring at most 0.2, in at least 4 of 5 seeds. If the gate fails, no pattern claim is made anywhere.
- (b) Word-length cap: train on an arc of L consecutive shifts, against L shifts spread at random, L in {3, 6, 9, 12}. Measure zero-shot by generator distance j, then j*(L) and L*.
- (c) Hatching: every 25 updates, measure zero-shot on unseen shifts and held-out accuracy on trained shifts, to see when the generator is extracted.
- (d) Apply T4b's erasures in this net (task identity at h1 and h2; the c-plane at z1), and test conjugation (c to -c) as the only meaningful inverse.

T4 Erasure and counter-steering (about 12 min)
- (a) At z1, as designed: edit, block, and push at half and full block damage. Reflection is labelled out-of-distribution.
- (b) T4b, after a nonlinearity: class-mean (LEACE) erasure of task identity at h1 and at h2.
  - Measures: forward accuracy; linear and MLP probes (task identity, and the answer pooled over tasks); relearning by column search with the erasure on.
  - Controls: damage-matched random subspaces.
- (c) Two-family world (add shifts and sub shifts): erase or block the add code. Measure forget (add) against retain (sub), relearning of the add tasks, and the probes from (b).

T5 Un-searching by INLP on the search (about 20 min)
- As designed, with these changes: 24 rounds; W = 128 and 256; best-of-budget accuracy at 300 and 3,000 steps.
- Variants: removal in the column space only, and the z1 block.
- Controls: random removals and top-PC removals; effects measured against a damage-matched random removal.
- Every round: side effects on the old tasks and on the probes.

T6 Trajectory primitives (about 8 min)
- Snapshot the task code every 25 updates and take the SVD against 50 shuffled-increment nulls.
- Keep a mode only if its variance exceeds the nulls' p99 and its time course departs from the cosine template more than the nulls' do.
- Causal cap: retrain with the task-column updates restricted to the top-L reference modes, L = 1-4.
- Measures: level 1, steps to 0.95, B2, and how much more E_x and W2 move than in the free run.

T7 PC (at most 45 min; after the Colab PC checkpoints exist)
- Use the PC arm as amended: β = 0.1, T = 20, truncated inference.
- Run T4b and T5 with the operations placed inside the energy, and the column search by the arm's own local update.
- Run 'relaxation restores task information' at T in {20, 100}.
- Claims only from paired PC-minus-BP differences whose CI excludes 0 and whose size is at least 0.10.

3 Qwen (block 14; same device as the RSI plan; the plan's keys; 90% prompt bootstrap; random directions isotropic and covariance-matched, at matched damage)

4 Rules (only the changes; everything else as designed)

Qwen, 'unlearned' requires all of:
- the instructed gap is at most 0.5 Delta_T, with the 90% upper end at most 0.75 Delta_T and below the random ablations' 5th percentile;
- D falls by at least 50%, with adjusted z at most -2.33;
- damage at most 0.05 nats;
- every foreign axis moves less than half as much as RSI.
A counter-push meeting the same thresholds is 'suppressed'. The shift in unprompted texts is reported but not required.

Qwen, re-search:
- 'Shallow' if the in-context cue restores at least 50% of the gap.
- 'Deep' if it restores less than 20%, with the upper end below 35%.
- Otherwise 'partial'.

Qwen, loop:
- 'Un-searched' if, at rounds 2-3, the ablated loop has p_n > 0.10 and |A_n| < 0.25 while its twin has p_n at most 0.01 and A_n at least 0.25.
- 're-routed' as designed.

Toy, per layer (T4b):
- 'causal': accuracy upper bound at most 0.11, with the damage-matched random control at least 0.9 x base;
- 'correlate': accuracy at least 0.9 x base, with the linear probe at most chance + 0.05;
- 'nonlinear residue': MLP probe at least chance + 0.05, with the CI excluding chance.

Toy, un-search (best-of-budget accuracy):
- 'Un-searched' if the upper bound is at most 0.11 while A_rand stays at least 0.8 x A_free.
- 'Partial' if at most 0.5 x A_rand.
- 'Specific' only against the retain family: retain accuracy at least 0.9 x its value under the damage-matched random operation.

Pattern:
- Only if the T3 gate passes.
- In the one-hot world, only if ring minus permuted ring is above 0 with the CI above 0. The pilot predicts it is below 0.

Word length:
- j*(L) = largest j with zero-shot accuracy of at least 0.5.
- L* = smallest L at which j* covers the whole circle in at least 4 of 5 seeds.
- A 'word-length effect' only if the arc arm is below the spread arm at equal L.

Trajectory:
- Only modes beyond the null count.
- 'Re-routing under the cap' if the capped run reaches level 1 while E_x and W2 move at least 1.5 times as much as in the free run.

Q1 verdicts are valid only if the planted calibration passes.
